Vision Therapy

Vision Therapy is a personalized program of eye exercises designed to improve visual skills and coordination.It helps treat problems like eye strain, poor focus, lazy eye, and binocular vision issues. The therapy enhances overall visual efficiency, making everyday tasks like reading and driving easier.

Dry Eye

Dry eye, or dry eye disease (DED), is a common condition where the tear film on the eye’s surface becomes unstable. This can cause irritation, discomfort, and even affect your vision.

Cataract

Cataracts are the most common cause of vision loss in people over age 40 and is the principal cause of blindness in the world.

Glaucoma

Glaucoma is an eye condition that damages the optic nerve, often due to high eye pressure. It usually affects older adults and can lead to vision loss without early symptoms. Regular eye checkups are important to detect it early and prevent blindness.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetic Retinopathy is an eye condition caused by high blood sugar damaging the blood vessels in the retina. It can lead to vision loss if not treated early.
